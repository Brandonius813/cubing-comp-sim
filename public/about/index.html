CUBING COMP SIM

Practice the whole round.

Simulate a Cubing Competition. Generate a scramble, inspect, solve, and work through a complete round at your own pace.

Start practicing →

Competition-style practice

Practice multiple puzzle events with inspection callouts, configurable waits, keyboard timing or manual entry, and a scorecard for each round.

Practice offline

Scrambles are generated on your device. After the app has downloaded its offline assets, you can keep practicing without a connection.

Your history, your choice

Use the simulator without an account. Your results stay in your browser unless you explicitly upload a cloud save. Accounts offer one current save, with manual upload and download.

Optional sign-in

Accounts let you keep a cloud copy of your practice history and transfer it between devices. Email, Google, and WCA sign-in are available. Social sign-in requests basic identity and email information to authenticate your account, not access to your inbox, calendar, or files.

Read how account information and practice data are handled in our Privacy Policy, and review the Terms of Service.
